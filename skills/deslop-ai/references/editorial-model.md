## 1. Evidence labels

- **A:** direct artifact or verifiable defect.
- **B:** measured corpus marker with domain/model limits.
- **C:** repeated field observation.
- **D:** established editorial problem.
- **E:** interpretive heuristic requiring judgment.

Single vocabulary or punctuation matches remain low-weight. Escalate clusters across independent families and substantive failures.

## 2. Editorial vector

Report `artifacts / specificity / support / structure / inflation / repetition / context-fit` on a 0–3 scale. Report value coverage separately. Never translate the vector into a probability of AI authorship.

## 3. Rewrite policy

Use conservative, meaning-preserving edits only. Safe deterministic edits may remove interface residue or redundant throat-clearing and simplify unambiguous bureaucratic forms. For unsupported significance, fake insight, missing evidence, or a vague headline, propose the required content rather than inventing it.

## 4. Fairness

Do not penalize non-native English, formal education, disability/accessibility tools, translation, house style, em dashes, triplets, or rhetorical contrast by presence alone. Explain observable reader harm. Abstain when authorship, genre, or context cannot be inferred safely.
